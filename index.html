Online Vehicle Service Booking & Cost Estimation System

A web-based system providing vehicle owners with instant cost estimation, customized maintenance advisories by vehicle type, and verified booking receipt generation.

Open Service Booking Form

System Capabilities

Overview of technical requirements implemented in this application

Real-Time Calculation

JavaScript calculates and updates estimated service totals on the page immediately when services are selected or deselected.

Dynamic Vehicle Advice

Selecting a vehicle type (Saloon, SUV, Pickup, or Van) dynamically updates tailored mechanical inspection advice without reloading.

Digit 6 Special Offer

Automatic bundle discount: Computer Diagnosis is waived to KSh 0 whenever Full Service is selected, reflected in both JS and PHP.

Server-Side Validation

Form inputs are sanitized and independently re-evaluated in PHP, outputting an official verified receipt.
